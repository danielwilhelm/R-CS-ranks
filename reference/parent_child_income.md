# Income of parents and children

An artificial dataset containing income of children and their parents
together with some information about them.

## Usage

``` r
parent_child_income
```

## Format

A data frame with 3894 rows and 4 variables:

- c_faminc:

  Family income of a child

- p_faminc:

  Family income of parent

- gender:

  Gender

- race:

  Race: hisp (Hispanic), black or neither
